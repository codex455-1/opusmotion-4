## Design rules

- One idea per frame; the voice carries the argument, the image carries the
  evidence.
- Movement is weighted: nothing snaps except the stamp, the shutter and the
  flash. Everything else uses long, damped eases.
- No music. No risers used as music. No whoosh without a physical source.
- The palette is ink, bone, sand, oxidised red and signal amber. Satellite
  imagery is graded to the same palette so archive and diagram share a world.
- Type is information, not decoration: mono for coordinates and instrument
  labels, Bodoni for the human/title register, Noto Serif SC for the two
  Chinese marks.
