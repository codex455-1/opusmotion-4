# Image-generation prompts (optional hero plates)

Every frame of the delivered film is generated by code (Skia vector art, GLSL, real OSM/Natural Earth geodata).
The plates below are **optional upgrades** for elements that a photoreal generator does better than procedural
drawing: organic materials, human silhouettes, and photographic depth. Each prompt is written to drop straight into
the existing engine as a texture or parallax layer.

**Global rules for every plate**
- Format: 9:16 vertical, 2160×3840 (downscaled to 1080×1920 in the engine). Keep the top 25% (the caption band, y < 480 at 1080p) calm, dark and low-detail.
- Palette ("archive noir"): ink #07090D, charcoal #161A21, warm paper #EAE2D0, cream #F3ECDD, signal red #D23A2E (sparingly), lamp amber #E9A64E, Thames teal #6FA3A8.
- Look: shot on 35 mm Kodak Vision3 500T, gentle halation, natural grain. Motivated practical light only. Deep blacks that are not crushed. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
- Ethics: no real person's likeness (Mitrokhin, Norwood or anyone else). Figures are anonymous, seen as a silhouette or from behind.
- Universal negative prompt: `text, letters, watermark, logo, signature, cartoon, anime, 3d render look, plastic, oversaturated, HDR halo, lens flare stars, extra limbs, deformed hands, modern objects, smartphones, LED lights, cluttered background`

### 1. The embassy doorway (replaces the S1 wide-shot background)
`Cinematic interior, a dim 1990s Baltic embassy entrance hall at dusk, symmetrical one-point perspective, tall double wooden doors at the centre thrown open, cold overcast daylight flooding in from outside and spilling a long trapezoid of light across a polished dark stone floor toward the camera, faint dust motes in the light shafts, plain panelled walls in near-darkness, a single elderly man in a worn overcoat and flat cap standing as a pure black silhouette in the doorway holding a scruffy shopping bag, his long shadow stretching toward the lens, camera at hip height, 24mm anamorphic, f/2.8, Kodak Vision3 500T, deep blacks, restrained cool palette with teal-grey highlights, quiet, tense, documentary realism, 9:16`
- Engine use: background plate under the procedural door-opening animation. Matte the doorway so the engine's light-spill and volumetric shafts stay live. Keep the silhouette on its own layer so the walk animation can drive it.

### 2. The scruffy bag — macro hero (S1 close-up)
`Macro product photograph of a battered 1980s Soviet brown vinyl-leather holdall bag standing on a dark walnut table, worn creases, scuffed corners, cracked handle straps, a brass zip, two cured salami sausages tied with string and a crusty baton loaf poking out of the open top, single warm tungsten lamp from the upper left, deep falloff into black, shallow depth of field, soft contact shadow, faint dust in the air, 100mm macro, f/4, rich browns and oxblood reds against near-black, cinematic still life, 9:16, bag centred in the lower 60% of frame`
- Engine use: replace `props.draw_bag` exterior with this plate. A second plate of the identical bag photographed as a clean **cutaway section** (prompt 2b) cross-dissolves under the existing amber reveal line.
- 2b: `…same bag, same light, technical museum cutaway: the front panel removed in a clean vertical section revealing inside, from top to bottom: salami, a bread loaf, a folded newspaper, and beneath them a thick neat stack of typed cream paper pages and kraft envelopes, precise, archival, 9:16`
