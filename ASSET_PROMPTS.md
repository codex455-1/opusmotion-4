# Image-generation prompt book

These are production-grade prompts for a still-image model (Midjourney v7,
Imagen, Flux, etc.) for assets that should not be faked with vector shapes.
They are written to sit inside the film's palette and grain, not to invent
documented hardware. Generate at the listed aspect, then crop to 1080 × 1920.

**Global look:** 1967 colour negative, Kodak Ektachrome/Kodak 5219 hybrid,
fine grain, gentle halation around highlights, slightly lifted blacks, warm
bone highlights and desaturated teal shadows; palette #07090a, #e9dfc8,
#c9a86a, #b4472f, #6f8f7d. No text, no logos, no modern objects. Avoid plastic
skin, HDR, drone-camera sharpness, digital noise reduction, lens flare spam.

---

## 1. Takhli before dawn — U-2 on the runway

**Aspect:** 9:16 vertical. **Lens:** 40 mm anamorphic, T2.8, low angle.

> A single Lockheed U-2 reconnaissance aircraft on a blacked-out Royal Thai
> Air Force runway at 04:40, May 1967. The aircraft is seen from the front
> three-quarter, long glider wings filling the top third of the frame, slim
> fuselage, no national markings, no squadron badge, canopy dark. A ground
> crewman in olive fatigues crouches at the left main gear with a red torch;
> his face is lost in shadow. Wet tarmac reflects two amber runway lamps and
> the pale blue of the pre-dawn sky. Thin ground mist, heat shimmer from the
> idling chase vehicle at the far right. Light: one soft amber practical at
> camera left, cold 5600 K sky fill, deep shadow. Composition: aircraft low in
> frame, enormous negative space of dark sky above for the title. Photographed
> on 35 mm colour negative, pushed one stop, fine grain, halation on the lamps,
> slight gate weave. No text, no insignia, no modern vehicles.

**Negative:** modern SUVs, runway markings, bright floodlights, HDR, CGI
gloss, visible pilot face, US flags, text, watermark.

## 2. The pilot — pressure-suit portrait, anonymous

**Aspect:** 4:5. **Lens:** 85 mm, T2, eye level.

> A Taiwanese U-2 pilot in a partial-pressure suit, seated in a canvas ready-
> room chair before a dawn mission, 1967. The helmet is off and held in both
> hands; the face is turned three-quarters away from camera and half-obscured
> by shadow, so he is not identifiable. Silver-grey pressure garment, black
> harness, a single chronograph on the left wrist, a folded map on the knee.
> A bare 40 W bulb above, cool window light from the left, dark green wall,
> scuffed linoleum. The mood is quiet and unheroic. Kodak Portra 400 pushed to
> 800, 35 mm, shallow depth of field, dust in the air, warm skin highlight
> against desaturated green shadow. No insignia, no name tape, no flags.

**Negative:** identifiable celebrity likeness, modern flight suit, helmet
logos, smile, cinematic teal-orange, text, medals.

## 3. Two sensor packages on the desert floor

**Aspect:** 9:16. **Lens:** 65 mm, T4, high angle looking down.

> Two matte-grey cylindrical instrument cases, each about two metres long,
> lying on cracked salt-clay at Lop Nur at first light, 1967. One case is
> half-buried; the other is upright, its thin whip antenna raised a metre,
> a small red indicator lamp just visible. The ground is pale grey-brown
> polygonal salt crust with wind-scoured sand, no vegetation, no footprints
> except a single line of boot prints leading away. Low raking sun from the
> right, long shadows, fine airborne dust. The cases look hand-built, with
> latches, rivets and a stencilled serial number that is illegible. Muted
> sand/bone palette, deep umber shadows, 35 mm colour negative, subtle
> halation, no text.

**Negative:** sci-fi pods, glowing blue lights, clean studio product shot,
people, vehicles, modern electronics, text.

## 4. H-6 release over Lop Nur

**Aspect:** 9:16. **Lens:** 300 mm, T5.6, aerial side-on.

> A Chinese H-6 bomber (Tupolev Tu-16 derivative) in bare metal and pale grey
> camouflage, seen from a parallel chase aircraft at 10,000 m over the Lop Nur
> salt basin, 17 June 1967. Bomb-bay doors open; a single large weapon with a
> blunt nose and four tail fins falls clear, small against the desert. The
> horizon is high in frame, the ground a pale grey-brown plain with dry lake
> polygons and a faint road. Thin high cloud, hard noon light, heat haze.
> Documentary aerial photography, 35 mm, slightly overexposed sky, fine grain,
> no national insignia, no text.

**Negative:** modern jet, missiles, multiple bombs, contrails, saturation,
video-game render, text, flags.

## 5. Taipei signals room, 1967

**Aspect:** 4:5. **Lens:** 35 mm, T2, available light.

> A small signals-intelligence receiving room in Taipei, July 1967, at night.
> Two reel-to-reel tape machines on a steel table, a rack of valve receivers,
> a paper seismograph drum, a Bakelite telephone, and a single desk lamp with
> a green shade. A woman technician in a white blouse and dark skirt stands
> with her back to camera, headphones around her neck, one hand on the tape
> reel. The room is close, humid, with cables across the floor and a fan
> blurred at the edge. Light: 2800 K desk lamp, cold fluorescent spill from a
> corridor, deep shadow. Kodak 5219, 35 mm, pushed two stops, grain visible in
> the shadows, halation on the lamp. No readable text, no logos.

**Negative:** modern computer, LCD screens, server room, clean minimalism,
identifiable face, text, neon.

## 6. Declassified file cover

**Aspect:** 4:5. **Lens:** 50 mm macro, top-down.

> A aged manila file folder on a dark steel desk, photographed from directly
> above at a slight angle, 1967. The cover is typed with a typewriter, but the
> text is illegible/out of focus; three heavy black redaction bars cross the
> lower half. A red "TOP SECRET" rubber stamp is half-visible at the upper
> right, cracked and uneven. Paper is foxed, edges soft, a metal paper clip,
> one hole-punch. Raking light from the left, dust on the surface, deep
> shadow. 35 mm macro, Portra 400, fine grain, no digital cleanliness.

**Negative:** crisp modern documents, readable words, barcodes, QR codes,
printer paper, text overlays.

## 7. Lop Nur from the edge of space

**Aspect:** 9:16. **Lens:** 50 mm, T8, high-altitude oblique.

> The Lop Nur salt basin seen from 21,000 m at dawn, looking north-west: a
> vast pale polygonal desert, dry lake bed with darker ancient shorelines,
> thin blue atmospheric limb at the top of frame, the curve of the Earth just
> implied. No aircraft, no contrails, no labels. The palette is bone, sand,
> dusty rose and deep indigo; the horizon glows amber. Photographed on 70 mm
> colour negative, very fine grain, low contrast, natural atmospheric haze.
> Composition: empty foreground salt crust, the horizon one third from the
> top, enormous negative space for type.

**Negative:** satellite CGI, unrealistic Earth curvature, stars in daylight,
clouds, cities, text.

## 8. Film leader / title texture

**Aspect:** 9:16. **Lens:** macro, no camera.

> A strip of 35 mm black-and-white film leader and aged dark paper, scanned
> at high resolution, with fine scratches, dust, a faint chemical stain and
> soft halation at the edges. Nearly black, with a narrow band of warm bone
> light along the left edge. No numbers, no letters, no sprocket holes in
> focus. Used as a title background; extremely subtle texture.

**Negative:** heavy grunge, torn paper collage, letters, logos, colour noise.

## Use notes

- Generate at 2× final size, crop to 1080 × 1920, then add 2–4% grain and a
  very soft bloom in the compositor. Do not sharpen.
- Keep all faces anonymous or turned away; this is a reconstruction, not a
  portrait of a real identified person.
- Do not use generated frames as evidence. They are atmosphere plates; every
  factual claim is carried by the diagrams, dates and narration.
